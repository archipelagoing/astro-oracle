    How the calculation works

    Natal planetary positions are calculated at your birth instant. The moving planets are scanned hourly for conjunctions, sextiles, squares, trines, and oppositions to those fixed positions, then crossings are refined to approximately one second. Events are selected by UTC month and displayed in your chosen timezone. This is a numerical crossing search, not a certified exhaustive ephemeris: very close station/tangent contacts can be missed. Dates between 1900 and 2100 are supported by this interface.
